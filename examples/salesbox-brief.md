# Q4 pipeline brief

AI workers handle the research, enrichment and outreach so your team spends its time on conversations that close.

#### AI-native lead generation

## What changed this quarter

We moved three manual workflows onto **AI workers** and cut time-to-first-touch from *four days* to under an hour.

- **Reduce manual work:** research, enrichment and list hygiene run continuously
- **Reach the right buyers:** intent signals rank accounts before reps see them
  - Website visits and content downloads
  - Hiring and funding events
- **Activate demand faster:** qualified leads route to reps within minutes

> **Note:** figures below are for the APAC region only. Global numbers follow in the November review.

## Results

| Metric | Q3 | Q4 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Qualified leads | 1,240 | 2,180 | +76% |
| Time to first touch | 4.1 days | 52 min | −98% |
| Meetings booked | 186 | 301 | +62% |
| Pipeline created | $3.2M | $5.9M | +84% |
